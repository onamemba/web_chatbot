# Promotion Process

## Overview
Promotions are evaluated twice per year, at the end of Q2 (June) and Q4 (December), aligned with the company's performance review cycles.

## Eligibility
To be considered for promotion, an employee generally must:
- Have been in their current role for at least 12 months.
- Have received a "Meets Expectations" or higher rating in their two most recent performance reviews.
- Demonstrate consistent performance at the next level's scope and responsibility for at least one full review cycle before the promotion is finalized.

## How the Process Works
1. **Self-assessment**: Employees complete a self-assessment highlighting achievements, scope of work, and readiness for the next level.
2. **Manager nomination**: Your manager reviews your self-assessment and, if they agree you're ready, submits a promotion packet to the calibration committee.
3. **Peer feedback**: 360-degree feedback is collected from peers, cross-functional partners, and direct reports (if applicable).
4. **Calibration committee review**: A panel of senior leaders reviews all promotion packets across the department to ensure consistency and fairness in leveling.
5. **Decision and communication**: Final decisions are communicated to managers first, who then share the outcome with employees individually, typically within 2 weeks of the calibration meeting.

## What to Do If You Want to Be Considered
- Talk to your manager early, ideally 2-3 months before the review cycle, about your promotion goals.
- Keep a running document of your accomplishments, impact, and scope throughout the year, don't wait until review time.
- Ask your manager for specific, actionable feedback on what's needed to reach the next level.

## If You're Not Promoted
Managers are expected to provide clear, constructive feedback on what to focus on for the next cycle. You can request a follow-up conversation with HR if you have concerns about the process or outcome.

## Questions
Contact your manager or hr@company.com for guidance on the promotion process.
