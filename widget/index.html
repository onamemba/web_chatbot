First, what is an LLM?

A large language model (LLM) is an AI trained on huge amounts of text. It writes fluent answers by predicting what words should come next.

It knows the world

Grammar, history, code, general facts. Great at explaining and summarizing.

It doesn't know you

Your prices, policies and services were never in its training data, so it can't quote them.

It can guess

Asked something it doesn't know, it may produce a confident answer that is wrong.

RAG gives the model an open book

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) looks up the relevant parts of your documents first, then asks the LLM to answer using only what it found.

1

Prepare

Your documents are split into small chunks and turned into numbers (embeddings) that capture meaning. They're stored in a vector database.

2

Retrieve

When someone asks a question, the system finds the few chunks closest in meaning to it.

3

Answer

The LLM gets the question plus those chunks and writes a reply grounded in them, naming the source.

documents → chunks → embeddings → vector database → LLM → answer

Because the knowledge lives in your files, updating the chatbot means editing a document. There is no retraining.

The chatbot

One pipeline, any subject. Swap the documents and the same chatbot becomes an HR helper, a support desk, a product guide, or a personal assistant for your business.

  • Answers only from your documents
  • Names the file each answer came from
  • Reads Markdown, text and PDF files
  • Says so when it doesn't know, and points to your contact
  • Fast replies with Groq-hosted models
  • Works as a Streamlit app or an embeddable widget

Where FastAPI fits

FastAPI is a Python framework for building web APIs. Here it turns the chatbot's brain into a service that any website can call.

A web page can't run Python, search a vector database, or safely hold your Groq key. So that work stays on a server, and the chat bubble on your site just sends it questions and displays what comes back.

Add it to any website

The chatbot runs as a small API. Your site only needs one line of HTML.

  1. Add your documents and index them

    Put files in data/ (or your S3 bucket), then run:

    python src/ingest.py

  2. Deploy the API

    Run it locally with uvicorn api:app --port 8000, or deploy the included Dockerfile to Render, Railway or similar. You'll get a public HTTPS address.

  3. Paste one line before </body>

    Works on plain HTML, React, Next.js, WordPress, Webflow, Bolt and more.

    <script src="https://YOUR-API-URL/static/chat-widget.js" defer></script>
Look at the bottom-right corner of this page. That chat bubble is the real widget. On your site it appears in the same place, and the title, color and side can be changed with data-title, data-color and data-position.
