# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Work Location Categories
- **Fully Remote**: Employee works from a home office or approved remote location full-time.
- **Hybrid**: Employee splits time between an office location and remote work, typically 2-3 days per week in-office as agreed with their manager.
- **Fully On-site**: Employee works from a company office location full-time.

Your work location category is defined in your offer letter and can only be changed with manager and HR approval.

## Equipment and Expenses
- The company provides a laptop and standard peripherals (monitor, keyboard, mouse) for remote employees.
- Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office stipend to cover desk, chair, or other setup costs, submitted via expense report within the first 90 days.
- Internet costs are not reimbursed unless required for a specific business need approved by your manager.

## Core Hours and Availability
Regardless of work location, employees are expected to be available and responsive during core hours of 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM in their local time zone, to support cross-team collaboration.

## Working from a Different Location Temporarily
Employees may work from a different location (e.g., visiting family, traveling) for up to 4 consecutive weeks per year without formal approval, as long as:
- Work hours remain aligned with core hours above.
- The location does not create tax, legal, or security complications (check with HR if working internationally).
- Manager is informed in advance.

## Questions
Contact your manager or hr@company.com for questions about your specific work arrangement.
