# Leave of Absence Policy

## Types of Leave
The company supports several types of leave beyond standard PTO:

### Medical Leave
Employees who need extended time off due to a serious health condition may request medical leave for up to 12 weeks under FMLA (if eligible) or company medical leave policy. Documentation from a healthcare provider is required.

### Parental Leave
New parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement) are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave. This can be taken continuously or split within the first 12 months after the child's arrival.

### Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days off following the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 2 paid days for extended family.

### Personal Leave of Absence
For circumstances not covered by other leave types, employees may request an unpaid personal leave of absence for up to 30 days, subject to manager and HR approval.

## How to Request a Leave of Absence
1. Notify your manager and HR as early as possible, ideally at least 2 weeks before the leave begins (except in emergencies).
2. Submit a formal Leave of Absence Request form through the HR portal.
3. Provide any required documentation (medical certification, adoption paperwork, etc.).
4. HR will confirm your eligibility, leave duration, and pay status within 5 business days.
5. Your job position (or an equivalent one) is protected during eligible leave periods per company policy and applicable law.

## Returning from Leave
Contact your manager and HR at least 1 week before your expected return date to coordinate your transition back to work.

## Questions
Contact hr@company.com or your HR business partner for guidance specific to your situation.
