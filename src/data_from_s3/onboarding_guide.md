# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Your First Day
- IT will ship your laptop and equipment to arrive 2-3 business days before your start date.
- You'll receive an email with login credentials for the HR portal, email, and Slack 1 business day before you start.
- Complete any pending new-hire paperwork (I-9, tax forms, direct deposit) in the HR portal before day one if possible.

## Day One
1. Log into the HR portal and complete your profile (emergency contact, benefits elections if not already done).
2. Attend the New Hire Orientation session (calendar invite sent automatically), which covers company culture, benefits overview, and key policies.
3. Meet with your manager for a 1:1 to review your first 30/60/90-day plan.
4. Set up your workstation: laptop, email, Slack, and any team-specific tools (your manager or IT buddy will guide you).

## First Week
- Complete required compliance training modules (Code of Conduct, Security Awareness, Anti-Harassment) via the Learning Portal, due within 14 days of your start date.
- Schedule introductory 1:1 meetings with key teammates and cross-functional partners (your manager will provide a suggested list).
- Review your team's documentation, current projects, and processes.

## First 30 Days
- Set initial goals with your manager for your first 30/60/90 days.
- Enroll in benefits (health, dental, vision, 401k) within 30 days of your start date, this is a hard deadline; missing it means waiting until the next open enrollment period unless you have a qualifying life event.
- Join relevant internal Slack channels and distribution lists for your team and interests.

## Who to Contact
- **IT issues**: it-support@company.com
- **HR/benefits questions**: hr@company.com
- **Payroll questions**: payroll@company.com
- **General onboarding questions**: Your assigned onboarding buddy or your manager

## Tips for Success
- Don't be afraid to ask questions, everyone expects a learning curve in the first few months.
- Document what you learn as you go; it'll help you and future new hires.
- Schedule regular check-ins with your manager, even informal ones, to stay aligned early on.
