# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Overview
All full-time employees accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) starting from their first day of employment. PTO can be used for vacation, personal time, or illness.

## Accrual Rates
- Employees with 0-2 years of tenure accrue 15 days (120 hours) of PTO per year.
- Employees with 3-5 years of tenure accrue 20 days (160 hours) of PTO per year.
- Employees with 6+ years of tenure accrue 25 days (200 hours) of PTO per year.

PTO accrues monthly and is credited to your account on the last day of each month.

## How to Request PTO
1. Log into the HR portal and navigate to "Time Off Requests."
2. Select the dates you wish to take off and the reason (vacation, personal, illness).
3. Submit the request at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences.
4. Your manager will approve or deny the request within 2 business days.
5. Once approved, the PTO will automatically be deducted from your balance.

For unplanned absences (e.g., sudden illness), notify your manager as soon as possible by phone or message, and submit the formal PTO request retroactively within 2 business days of returning to work.

## Carryover and Payout
- Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Any unused PTO beyond the carryover limit is forfeited at year-end unless local law requires payout.
- Upon termination or resignation, accrued and unused PTO (up to the carryover limit) will be paid out in the final paycheck.
